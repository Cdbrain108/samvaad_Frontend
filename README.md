## 🌸 Overview

**Samvaad (संवाद)** is an independent, non-commercial educational seva and portfolio project built to demonstrate how state-of-the-art **Agentic AI**, **Domain Fine-Tuning**, and **Multi-Source Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** can be harmonized with timeless Vedic wisdom.

It bridges modern natural language intelligence with classical scriptures, giving seekers a deeply personal, compassionate, and authentic spiritual mentor experience.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights

### 📜 Multi-Source Scriptural RAG (150K+ Sacred Verses)
Rather than hallucinating answers, Samvaad is anchored in authentic scriptural foundations:
- **150,000+ Verses Indexed**: Comprehensive coverage of **25+ sacred scriptures**, including the **Shrimad Bhagavad Gita**, **Valmiki Ramayana**, **Shri Ramcharitmanas**, the **18 Maha-Puranas** (Matsya, Garuda, Bhagavata, Shiva, Vishnu, etc.), **Upanishads**, and the **Vedas**.
- **Hybrid Semantic Retrieval**: Contextual dense vector embeddings and keyword matching retrieve chapter-accurate shlokas, Sanskrit verses, and classical meanings for genuine spiritual grounding.

### 🧘 Fine-Tuned Domain Intelligence (Gemma 4 E4B IT)
- Fine-tuned on **4,000+ authentic Bhajan Marg discourses** and **50,000+ specialized spiritual Q&A pairs**.
- Captures the gentle, fatherly, and compassionate counseling tone (*"राधे राधे बच्चा"*) characteristic of Pujya Maharaj Ji’s satsangs.
- Balances stern philosophical discipline with deeply comforting emotional solace.

### 🔍 Intelligent Intent Understanding & Pre-Flight Gating
- A dedicated **User Understanding Agent** evaluates every incoming query before calling external systems.
- **Spiritual Inquiries** (*e.g., "What is Maya?", "How to conquer grief?"*) activate the deep scriptural RAG pipeline with verified citations.
- **Personal / Emotional Dilemmas** (*e.g., "I feel restless", "My name is Anuj"*) receive direct, heartfelt satsang counseling without forced or unnatural verse insertion.

### 🕯️ Live Spiritual Deliberation (Thinking Process)
- **4-Stage Contemplative Stepper**: Illuminates real-time reasoning stages:
  1. *Analyzing Seeker Intent & Inner State*
  2. *Retrieving Grounded Verses across 150K+ Scriptures*
  3. *Deliberating Maharaj Ji’s Satsang Counsel*
  4. *Harmonizing Sacred Verses with Divine Solace*
- **Wisdom Pearls**: Summarizes the contemplative essence into an illuminated aphorism (*चिंतन का पावन सूत्र*).

### ☀️ Dual Day & Night Sacred Temple Aesthetic
- **Day Mode**: Warm sandalwood, ivory parchment, illuminated marigold highlights, and classical folio typography.
- **Night Mode**: Deep midnight temple sanctum, soft diya oil lamp flickers, and sacred golden halos.
- **Palm-Leaf Manuscript Storytelling (तालपत्र पोथी)**: Interactive storytelling celebrating ancient palm-leaf preservation heritage.

### ⚡ Zero-Cost Cloud CPU Infrastructure
- Engineered to achieve fast, high-quality local and remote inference on **free open-source infrastructure**.
- Powered by 4-bit quantized GGUF execution on an **Oracle Cloud Free Tier CPU VM**, demonstrating that modern Agentic AI systems do not require expensive enterprise GPU clusters.
